# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruptive conduct.

Maintain respectful discussion, assume good intent while addressing impact, and focus technical disagreement on evidence. Maintainers may remove content or contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's contact mechanisms. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/); the response process is handled by the maintainers.
